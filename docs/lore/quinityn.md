# Quinityn: a world under contract

[Documentation index](../README.md) · [Historical sources](research.md)

A forgotten contract world. An ocean of unicomp. An industry waiting to restart.

Quinityn's name comes from **YuokiTani's original fiction**, introduced on the
[Factorio forums on December 1, 2015](https://forums.factorio.com/viewtopic.php?t=18145).
The two German-language stories describe an industrial frontier shaped by
corporate contracts, stranded workers, hostile life and interworld commerce.
Old equipment is another opportunity to do business with the people below.

The [historical research](research.md#original-fiction-why-quinityn-is-the-right-name)
summarizes both stories and links their original PDFs. It also traces Yuoki's
materials, machines, signs and trade systems through primary sources.

## This add-on's Quinityn

The add-on imagines the world after its great industrial works have fallen
silent. Weathered soil and dead turf survive between ash, slag and buried
machinery. Purple unicomp seas surround ruined districts, and poisoned trees
grow among the remains. The machinery is salvage; the sea can become the
foundation of a new industry.

The unicomp oceans, this landscape, flyash discovery and the staged planetary
research journey are **new interpretations for this add-on**. They are not
claims about what YuokiTani wrote. The game lets you build and launch a rocket;
it does not enforce the stories' contract prison or return you to the surface
after an escape.

## The Uni-touched

Long after the foundries stopped, native life continued feeding on the waste.
Living beside the unicomp waters altered Quinityn's biters, spitters, worms
and nests, giving them their distinctive violet shades. The industrial world
has become part of its inhabitants.

## The Stomp-a-trons

Unicomp waters and generations of N4 and F7 consumption transformed these
creatures, but their mechanical hearts tell an older story. In the planet's
industrial past, a forgotten experiment grafted a living stomper to a
Spidertron chassis. The works are dust; the experiment's descendants endure,
carrying mineral armor, scavenged machinery and fragments of ancient memory.

This origin, the Uni-touched identity and their appearance are **Quinityn add-on
lore**, not part of the original stories. For their actual gameplay behavior,
sizes and loot, read the [enemy field guide](../guides/enemies.md).
