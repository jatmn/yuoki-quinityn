# Yuoki Industries: Quinityn

A Factorio **2.1 + Space Age** planet addon for **Yuoki Industries and Yuoki Industries Engines**. Developed for jatmn.

Quinityn is the planet named in YuokiTani's original stories. This interpretation gives it volcanic industrial terrain, salvage, native biters, and purple seas of **actual Yuoki Liquid Unicomp A2**. Oceans accept offshore pumps and destroy discarded items using Factorio's native lava disposal mechanic.

Yuoki and Engines recipes are locked until a character physically lands on Quinityn. A force-wide technology tree then guides materials, power, Cimota reconstruction, Mechanical Force, refining, farming, defense, trade and advanced industry. Landing with an empty inventory is supported; a fresh force can also progress from zero research. The mod does not change your initial spawn planet.

## Development status

Dependencies currently come from the pending 1.3.0 branches:

- [Engines 2.1 PR #3](https://github.com/jatmn/Yuoki-Engines-Factorio-2.0/pull/3), commit `dd13f42`.
- Factorio / Space Age 2.1.21 or later.

No upstream branches are modified by this addon. The currently published 2.0 versions cannot substitute for these 2.1 builds.

## Play

Research Planet discovery Quinityn after the normal Space Age prerequisites and travel from Nauvis. Landing opens the field survey recipes. See the [local progression guide](docs/progression.md) and the technology descriptions in game.

Installation and reproducible builds are described in [development](docs/development.md). Historical evidence, lore boundaries and sources are in [research](docs/research.md); the [forum index](docs/forum-index.json) records the archive coverage. New geography and gameplay are an adaptation, not claims that the 2015 stories described unicomp oceans.
