---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-07
  Features:
    - Added Quinityn, a Yuoki contract world inspired by the original stories.
    - Added liquid unicomp seas with native item disposal, N4/F7 resources, salvage and biters.
    - Added landing-gated Yuoki and Engines technology progression and local bootstrap recipes.
    - Added local petrochemical research and Yuoki rocket component alternatives.
    - Added 59 native Tips and Tricks progression chapters.
    - Added Durotal foundations for exclusive unicomp expansion and normal foundation destinations.
    - Added planet-exclusive science and infinite mining productivity / plasma damage research.
    - Added smog, slag districts, buried machinery and low pollution absorption.
    - Limited starting ore and made emergency resource conversions hand-crafting only.
  Changes:
    - Restricted new cliffs to machinery-covered ruined districts; standardized Quinityn generation-control names.
    - Restricted map-generated nests and worms to brown slag, preserving later expansion onto other walkable terrain.
    - Opened gaps in roughly half the coastal cliffs; slightly increased and brightened purple dead-tree patches.
    - Replaced red-only N4/F7 bulk research with nine crafting milestones; science unlocks after making the first factory.
    - Joined landing terrain to a winding dry land connection, with clear passage for biter attacks.
    - Replaced inland cliff bands with Fulgora-style coastal cliffs and occasional inland escarpments.
    - Added sparse purple dead/decaying trees with independent frequency, coverage and disable controls.
    - Anchored the field survey and Yuoki tree beneath planet discovery; require Quinityn science after crafting milestones.
    - Split bulk unlocks into production and machine upgrade tiers with representative research icons and guide entries.
    - Added independent unicomp coverage/scale, default-on cliffs and Quinityn enemy-base controls.
    - Enlarged the bottle badge for 32-pixel inventory readability.
    - Reworked coastlines into smaller organic, mostly connected industrial districts; removed the forced starter pond.
    - Enlarged and randomized starting N4/F7 patches to 125,000–150,000 units each.
    - Clustered collapsed stockpiles into sparse districts with useful starter salvage and reduced overall placement density.
    - Added ash, rubble, rocks, cracks, pumice and machinery debris.
    - Added original salvage and purple science-bottle icons with a larger, readable Technic Sign badge.
    - Restricted planet science to the three Yuoki factories; placed the first factory before its separate science unlock.
    - Added expensive factory-only Technic Sign qualification without byproducts.
    - Replaced the separator with a primitive Cimota appearance, at 0.5 crafting speed; raw F7 burns for 1 MJ.
  Fixes:
    - Limited N4/F7 map-generator planet badges to Quinityn without restoring ore generation elsewhere.
    - Matched the hand-sorting recipe icon to its salvage input and added direct guide/tooltip links.
    - Added missing localized surface units and custom prototype/interface names.
  Compatibility:
    - Existing completed technologies remain; recipes moved into new upgrade technologies require that new research.
    - Existing separator IDs are preserved. Existing generated terrain/ore is not rewritten; use a new map to see the revised starting area.
    - Targets the pending Yuoki and Engines 1.3.0 Factorio 2.1 branches.
    - Preserves upstream unlock prerequisites and the heavy-oil conversion opt-out.
    - Disables the optional starting suit to preserve the physical-visit gate.
  Testing:
    - Added multi-seed native nest-to-landing paths across default and extreme liquid settings.
    - Added factory operation, F7 fuel, biter collision/pathfinding, localization and terrain-distribution checks.
    - Added official headless engine fixtures, local resource closure and finite bootstrap budget checks.
