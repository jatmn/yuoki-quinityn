Version: 0.1.2
Date: 2026-11-02
  Bugfixes:
    - Group the Primitive Cimota with its machine family, industrial salvage with its sorting process, and Technic Sign qualification with signs in Factoriopedia. Keep manual resources together ahead of the separate Cimota conversion rows.
    - The Primitive Cimota Restructor can now be upgraded to the Cimota Restructor with upgrade planners (including a blank planner), upgraded blueprints, construction robots and hand fast-replacement.     - The Primitive Cimota recipe picker now lists conversions in two rows, as the full Cimota does: stone and wood into UC-A2 first, then coal, copper ore, iron ore and water out of it.
